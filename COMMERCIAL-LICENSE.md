## Do I need a commercial license?

The AGPL lets you use, modify and ship track-changes for free, **on condition
that the application you ship it in is also released as open source under the
AGPL**. A web app sends this code to users' browsers, so the condition
applies to web apps too: you must offer your users the complete source code
of the application that uses it.

| Your situation | License |
|---|---|
| Open-source project released under the AGPL | AGPL, free |
| Personal project, research or teaching, with source published under the AGPL | AGPL, free |
| Commercial product or internal tool whose source you don't publish | **Commercial** |
| SaaS, client work or any closed-source app | **Commercial** |
| Evaluating or prototyping, not yet shipped | AGPL, free |

If you're unsure, ask. We're happy to help.

## What the commercial license gives you

- Use in closed-source and commercial products, without the AGPL's
  source-sharing obligations
- No requirement to publish your application's code
- A license that covers the versions you buy, with terms that fit your
  company

## How to get one

Open a [commercial license request](https://github.com/carsonhung/track-changes/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml)
on GitHub. Issues are public, so include only your company name and a contact
route. We'll follow up privately with terms and pricing.
